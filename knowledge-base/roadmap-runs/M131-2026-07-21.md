# Milestone M131 — Fix #135 (columnar-agg EXPLAIN deparse recursion)

## Outcome

RELEASED as v0.117.0 (PR #139, merge commit c52ac89). Root cause established by a LIVE gdb backtrace, which
FALSIFIED the #135 hypothesis: the hang was an infinite recursion in `ruleutils.c::resolve_special_varno` during
EXPLAIN **deparse** (plan printing) — not the planner, not execution, not O(cols²)/wide-table. Trigger:
`ORDER BY <aggregate>` above the swapped CustomScan. Fix: a deparse-safe, descriptor-equal, fail-closed
`custom_scan_tlist`.

MEASURED on the shipped binary: 43-query ClickBench EXPLAIN sweep hangs 2 -> 0 (max 60 ms; Q16 31 ms, Q33 30 ms,
both engaging the CustomScan); accelerated ClickBench byte-identical 43/43 vs heap, 1.90x full-suite hot geomean
and 24.8x across the six queries the pushdown touches (20.7x excluding the zone-map-served q6). Honest: self-hosted
NOT canonical hardware, 100k subsample, single suite run per configuration, +-21% noise floor disclosed; internal
A/B of TheoDB against itself, not a competitive claim.

Reviews: council-rust-pgrx + council-benchmark, both 0 BLOCKER / 0 HIGH; 1 HIGH + 8 MEDIUM/LOW all resolved and
re-verified after rebuild. Side finding: the "zombie backends" that confused M128 were backends stuck in this loop.
CI was red for infra reasons throughout (filed as #140); verification came from measured droplet runs.
