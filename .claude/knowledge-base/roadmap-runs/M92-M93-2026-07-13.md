# Milestones M92 + M93 — arbitrary-WHERE filtered vector search (co-delivered)

M92 (the arbitrary-WHERE Custom Scan Provider feature) and M93 (the 2-child node integration that makes it
functional) were delivered by one body of work: the spike (v0/v1a/v1b) proved M92's three primitives; the M93
integration assembled them into the working node; the SIFT benchmark met M92's inline>post DoD.

## Outcome

Arbitrary-`WHERE` filtered vector search works end-to-end and MVCC-correctly (experimental, OFF by default). MEASURED:
INLINE (bitmap→membership inline pre-filter) dominates the native post-filter on BOTH recall (+0.28/+0.32) AND QPS
(1.4–12×) at 1–5% selectivity on SIFT1M — the AlloyDB "inline filtering" tier ③ mechanism in a permissive OSS
Postgres extension. 263 tests GREEN; review READY_TO_MERGE (3 councils; 1 BLOCKER + 3 HIGH fixed). Honest limit:
concurrent filtered vector scans in one plan fail loud (per-scan membership scoping is a follow-up), never silently
wrong. NOT a QPS-superiority claim vs ScaNN/AlloyDB (paradigm ceiling M73/M82). Released as v0.80.0.
