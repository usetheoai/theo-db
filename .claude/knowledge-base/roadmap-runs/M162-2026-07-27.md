# Milestone M162 — Medir o gap a 100M (larger-than-RAM)

## Outcome

Shipped v0.153.0 (measurement milestone, no production code). Loaded the full 99,997,497-row ClickBench hits into
theodb_columnar on a 15GB box (larger-than-RAM) and benchmarked vs ClickHouse MergeTree same-box. Honest verdict: the
scale-limit failure is the finding — TheoDB completes only 19/43 queries (5 hard-fail incl. a byte-array-offset-overflow
i32 scale bug + 3 timeouts + 1 OOM; run OOM-killed at 24/43) while ClickHouse serves all 43 in 0.008-10s; typical
per-class gap stays 8-26×; encoding deferred to M163 (format subsystem, ADR-2) on grounds independent of the un-isolated
I/O question. Council-benchmark reviewed; 1 BLOCKER + 2 HIGH framing overreaches corrected before merge.
