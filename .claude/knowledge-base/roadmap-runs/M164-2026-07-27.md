# Milestone M164 — Endurecer o harness de benchmark

## Objective (from ROADMAP.md)

Tornar o harness de medição auto-guardado contra os falso-verdes e a inadequação de infra que custaram horas (retro
items B+C de M160-M162): (B) `_ensure_sample` afirma `linhas_carregadas == n`; o A/B afirma que o braço ON de fato
roteou por EXPLAIN, não só `diverged=0`; (C) pre-flight que RECUSA/avisa um load quando RAM/disco é inadequado.

## Outcome

Shipped in v0.155.0. Three pure env-injected guards on `benchmarks/run_m128_clickbench.py`: `sample_is_fresh`
(count integrity, clamped to `HITS_TOTAL_ROWS`), `plan_shows_agg_pushdown` + `classify_ab` + `decide_ab_verdict`
(agg-specific A/B routing so a declined agg over the always-on projection scan can't pass as a trivial `diverged=0`),
and `preflight_sizing` (full-footprint disk BLOCK, larger-than-RAM WARN). 20 unit tests, no DB, ruff-clean. Two council
reviewers found — and the fix closed — a false-green inside the anti-false-green milestone itself (HIGH-2: the projection
CustomScan conflated with the agg pushdown). Both signed off READY_TO_MERGE in round 2.
