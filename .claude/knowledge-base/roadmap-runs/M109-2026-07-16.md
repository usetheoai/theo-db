# Milestone M109 — Grafo Fase 2: MS-BFS vetorizado

## Outcome
Vectorized Multi-Source BFS operator shipped as v0.98.0. Batched MS-BFS (up to 64 lanes/CSR
sweep via per-vertex u64 source-masks, frontier-driven) beats N sequential single-source BFS
~5–8× (pure traversal, mean±std, confound-free), ~10× on uniform topology (not hub-gamed),
oracle PASS every N=1..512. Deep research (Then VLDB'14, DuckPGQ, HippoRAG) corrected the
ROADMAP "reuse ah.rs kernels" misframing AND caught a row-materialization benchmark confound
that had masked the win (first measurement showed 0.44× — a measurement artifact, not reality).
