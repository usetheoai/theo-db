## Shipped

- **M45** — rigorous mean±std recall×QPS Pareto: `theodb_hnsw` vs `pgvector hnsw` on SIFT1M. An HONEST
  NEGATIVE (measurement-first): the milestone set out to confirm a vector-superiority claim and instead
  REFUTED the prior M42 signal. Under proper mean±std (500 queries, ≥3 timed runs, exact GT, matched build
  params m=16/efc=64, shared ef grid, fair `maintenance_work_mem` for pgvector), the verdict is **PARITY** —
  the M42 "~1.7–2.8× superiority" (best-of-N + 200-query + warm-cache) does NOT reproduce. Two runs gave
  INFERIOR→PARITY: the margin sits within run-to-run noise; the frontiers interleave (theodb faster at
  low-mid recall, pgvector at high recall). theodb_hnsw is **competitive, not superior**, vs pgvector hnsw
  at 1M. The M42 claim is retracted in three surfaces (doc, ROADMAP, CHANGELOG). North Star P0 (vector
  superiority vs the reachable SOTA) is **NOT met — it is parity**; next lever is theodb scan latency +
  variance. New: `benchmarks/{m45_pareto.py (pure, 17 unit tests), m45_report.py, run_m45_pareto.py}` +
  tests. Zero product-code change; reuses `theodb_bench.{dataset,db,recall}`. Delivers `public-copy.md`
  §4 half 1 (reproducible artifact); half 2 (independent reproduction) declared OPEN. Closes the M32
  mean±std ≥3-runs DoD bullet. Artifact: `docs/benchmarks/m45-pareto-sift1m.{md,json}`.
  Review: council-benchmark PASS + cross-validation READY_TO_MERGE + test-auditor (1 HIGH + MEDIUMs, all
  fixed — the effect>variance gate is now isolated by test, and `_margin_at` uses the frac-weighted
  interpolated std so a noisy bracket can't ride an understated variance into a false SUPERIOR).
