## Scope

M15 turns TheoDB from "a pile of init-scripts" into a real product: the AI + vector surface (the 12
`docs/features/` capabilities) installs as a versioned, upgradeable PostgreSQL extension —
`CREATE EXTENSION theodb CASCADE` — on any PostgreSQL 17. SQL-only PGXS umbrella (pgvector model, not pgrx),
`requires='vector, vectorscale, plpython3u'`, `superuser=true`. The image installs the extension at init
(replacing the six `docker-entrypoint-initdb.d` scripts). Anchored on the discovery blueprint
`pg-extension-packaging` (SHIPPABLE_WITH_CAVEATS).
